## 📊 Dataset Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Dataset** contains **1,500 order line items** (across 500 unique customer orders) representing retail operations in India.

The dataset contains information about:

- Order identifiers
- Sales transaction amounts
- Net profit/loss values
- Quantity sold per item
- Product categories
- Product sub-categories
- Customer payment methods

### Dataset Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `order_id` | Unique identifier linking the transaction line item to a specific order |
| `amount` | Total transaction sale amount/revenue for the item(s) in INR (₹) |
| `profit` | Net profit or loss generated from the item transaction in INR (₹) |
| `quantity` | Number of units purchased for the specific item |
| `category` | High-level product category (*Clothing, Electronics, Furniture*) |
| `sub_category` | Specific product type (*Printers, Sarees, Chairs, Furnishings, etc.*) |
| `payment_mode` | Payment method used (*COD, Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, EMI*) |

## 💼 Business Problem

An e-commerce retailer is experiencing uneven profit margins across product lines and payment options. While overall transaction volume is growing, management wants to pinpoint where profit leaks are occurring and which product categories yield the highest returns.

Management wants to understand:

> **Which products and payment modes drive overall revenue and net profit, and how can the store eliminate losses while maximizing overall margin?**

You have been brought in as a data analyst to investigate the transaction line-item data.

Your analysis should help the company understand its sales operations and identify operational and catalog patterns that require executive attention.

---

## 🔎 Your Task

Explore the dataset and use your findings to understand the drivers of sales revenue, unit volume, and profitability.

There is **no single correct approach** to this challenge.

You are encouraged to ask your own questions and investigate patterns that interest you.

### 🌱 If You're a Beginner

You can start with questions such as:

- What is the total revenue and total profit generated?
- Which product category brings in the highest revenue?
- What is the most frequently used payment method?
- Which sub-category has the highest total profit?
- How many total items were sold across all orders?

### 📈 If You're Intermediate

You can investigate questions such as:

- What is the overall profit margin percentage across different product categories?
- Are any sub-categories running at a net financial loss?
- Does payment method choice correlate with average transaction size or profitability?
- How do sales volumes (quantity) compare against actual revenue generated per category?
- Which sub-categories generate high sales volume but low or negative net returns?

### 🚀 If You're Advanced

You can take the analysis further by exploring:

- Profitability margin distributions across product sub-categories
- Outliers and high-risk product lines causing margin erosion
- Order aggregation analysis (combining line items back to order-level insights)
- Multi-factor category and payment gateway contribution analysis
- Discount/pricing efficiency metrics
- Other financial patterns you discover independently

**You do not have to answer every question listed above.**

The questions are provided as starting points. We want to see your ability to **ask questions, investigate the data, and find the story behind it.**
